| Dataset Split | Mode | Recall (<50) | Recall (<75) | FP Rate (<50) | FP Rate (<75) | ROC AUC [95% CI] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dev Set (Tuned, N=70)** | Rules-Only | 8.6% (3/35) | 40.0% (14/35) | 0.0% (0/35) | 0.0% (0/35) | 0.8073 [0.7081, 0.8936] |
| **Dev Set (Tuned, N=70)** | Rules + AI | 71.4% (25/35) | 100.0% (35/35) | 0.0% (0/35) | 2.9% (1/35) | 0.9988 [0.9950, 1.0000] |
| **Test Set (Sealed, N=50)** | Rules-Only | 0.0% (0/25) | 36.0% (9/25) | 12.0% (3/25) | 16.0% (4/25) | 0.6704 [0.5256, 0.8084] |
| **Test Set (Sealed, N=50)** | Rules + AI | **72.0% (18/25)** | **96.0% (24/25)** | **32.0% (8/25)** | **32.0% (8/25)** | **0.7560 [0.5974, 0.8908]** |

### Stated Dev-to-Test Gaps:
1. **Scam Recall Generalization**: Rules+AI achieved **72.0% recall at Trust < 50** and **96.0% recall at Trust < 75** on the sealed test set, closely matching dev recall (71.4% and 100.0%). The AI layer successfully lifted recall on test from 0% (rules-only) to 72% (+72.0% absolute gain).
2. **False-Positive Divergence (The Author Gap)**: While FP rate on dev was 0.0% (0/35), the FP rate on test was 32.0% (8/25). Crucially, **all 8 false positives on test occurred among the 10 owner-written genuine items (80.0% FP rate on owner items vs 0.0% on assistant items)**. The owner items were drafted with highly adversarial, urgent linguistic framing resembling actual scams, triggering both rules and AI classifiers.
3. **ROC AUC Generalization**: ROC AUC increased from 0.6704 (rules-only test) to **0.7560** (rules+AI test) [+0.0856], though below the dev ROC AUC (0.9988), driven by the elevated scores on owner-written hard genuine items.
